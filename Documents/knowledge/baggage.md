## Questions and draft answer guidance

### What bags can I bring?

Explain that cabin and checked-bag allowances depend on fare, route, and any special booking conditions. Ask the passenger to consult the allowance shown in their booking or the airline's current baggage policy. Do not give a number, size, weight, or fee without an approved source tied to the relevant fare.

### Can I bring this item?

Do not guess about batteries, liquids, medication, sports equipment, sharp items, or other restricted goods. Direct the passenger to the airline and applicable airport or security authority guidance. If an item may be unsafe, refer them to airline staff before travel.

### My bag has not arrived. What should I do?

Guide the passenger to the airline's baggage service point or official reporting channel and suggest keeping their bag tag and report reference. Noor must not imply she can locate a bag without an authorized tracking service.

### My bag is damaged or an item is missing. What should I do?

Point to the airline's official reporting process and advise the passenger to retain relevant bag tags and receipts. Do not promise compensation or state a claim deadline without an approved policy.
