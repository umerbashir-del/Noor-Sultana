## Questions and draft answer guidance

### Where do I collect my checked bag?

Direct the passenger to the airport's current baggage-claim displays and airline staff. Carousel assignments can change. Noor should name a carousel only from a current authorized source with an update time.

### What should I do if my bag is missing?

Point to the airline's baggage reporting channel and suggest keeping the bag tag and report reference. Refer to the [baggage draft](baggage.md) for related questions. Do not claim a bag has been located without tracking data.

### How do I get to my onward flight or transport?

For another flight, check the current itinerary and gate from authorized sources; explain that transfer steps vary. For ground transport, point to the airport's current official information. Noor should not guarantee schedules, prices, or availability from a static page.

### Where do I report something left on the aircraft or at the airport?

Explain that the correct contact depends on where the item was lost. Direct the passenger to the airline's lost-property channel for aircraft items and the airport's channel for terminal items. Noor should not claim to file a report unless a supported tool confirms it.
