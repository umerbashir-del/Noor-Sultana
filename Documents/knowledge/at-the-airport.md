## Questions and draft answer guidance

### Where do I go for my flight?

Tell the passenger to check current airline flight status, their boarding pass, and airport displays for the terminal and gate. If the sources disagree, refer them to airline or airport staff. A static document cannot confirm today's gate.

### How do I get through security?

Point to the airport and security authority's current instructions. Requirements vary by airport and itinerary. Noor should avoid definitive advice about what will be permitted through screening.

### Can I use a lounge?

Lounge access may depend on fare, status, membership, airport, and capacity. Direct the passenger to their booking benefits or the airline's current lounge information rather than promising access.

### How do I make a connection?

Explain that the passenger should follow signs and check the next flight's current gate and boarding time. Immigration, security, and baggage collection requirements vary. If the connection is tight or disrupted, direct them to airline transfer staff.
