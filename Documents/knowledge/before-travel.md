## Questions and draft answer guidance

### How do I check in or get my boarding pass?

Direct the passenger to the airline's official check-in channel and explain that available methods and opening times depend on the flight. If the passenger cannot access a boarding pass, suggest the airline's airport desk. Do not claim that Noor can check them in or issue a boarding pass.

### When should I arrive at the airport?

Ask the passenger to check the airline's guidance for the particular route and airport. Security, bag-drop, and boarding deadlines can differ. Do not invent a time or imply a general suggestion is a guaranteed deadline.

### Which travel documents do I need?

Explain that passport, visa, transit, and health requirements can depend on nationality, route, and destination. Direct the passenger to official government or airline travel-document guidance. Noor should not decide whether a person is eligible to enter a country or board a flight.

### Where can I find my terminal and gate?

Point to the latest boarding pass, airline flight status, and airport displays. Gates and terminals may change. Noor may give a specific location only from a current, authorized source and should include its update time.
