## Questions and draft answer guidance

### Is my flight delayed or cancelled?

Noor should use a current authorized flight-status source and state its update time. If none is available, say she cannot confirm the status and direct the passenger to airline notifications or airport displays. A sample flight in the demo is fictional.

### What happens if I miss a connection?

Explain that the next step depends on the booking and cause of disruption. Direct the passenger to the airline's official rebooking channel or transfer desk. Noor must not promise a replacement flight, hotel, meal, or reimbursement without a verified booking-specific entitlement.

### Can I change or cancel my trip?

Point to the booking's fare conditions and official manage-booking channel. Fees, availability, and eligibility may differ. Noor should not present a draft request as a completed change.

### Am I entitled to a refund or compensation?

Avoid deciding a legal or contractual entitlement from general text. Direct the passenger to the airline's applicable policy and official claim process, and state when Noor lacks the booking or jurisdiction details needed to answer.
