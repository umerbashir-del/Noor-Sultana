# AI agent OC — character consistency guide

Use the **turnaround** for proportions and the **outfit details** sheet for clothing construction. If generated panels disagree, this guide is the canonical design.

## Face and hair

- Pakistani adult woman with warm brown skin, large brown eyes, thick graceful eyebrows, a soft oval face, and small rose lips.
- Tiny silver stud on her **right nostril**; small simple ear studs.
- Long black hair arranged in broad curls, with a side-swept forelock, a few indigo highlight shapes, and one blue clip near her **left temple**.
- Keep the eye spacing, nose size, lip width, and hair mass consistent when the head turns.

## Canonical outfit

1. **Ivory blouse:** pointed collar, simple front buttons, tucked into the trousers.
2. **Indigo long coat:** tailored shoulders, fitted waist, below-knee hem, clean back center seam. One emerald panel with sparse antique-gold embroidery runs down her **right front opening**. The sleeve linings turn back to show coral cuffs.
3. **Cream drape:** a narrow dupatta-like scarf hangs from her **right shoulder**, with spaced indigo diamond motifs and a patterned lower border.
4. **Charcoal trousers:** high waist, dark belt with gold buckle, wide straight legs.
5. **Footwear:** dark ankle boots with modest block heels.
6. **Accessories:** gold compass-star pin on her **left lapel**; slim gold watch on her **left wrist**; small blue hair clip; slim navy notebook. Keep these in the same places in every view.

## Acting rules

- Lead a thought with the eyes, then brows and head, then shoulders or hands. The mouth follows the thought or speech.
- Use asymmetric brows and changes in gaze direction; avoid holding a fixed smile through every line.
- Let the long curls and cream drape lag behind head turns and settle after the body.
- Match the isolated **eyes** and **mouths** sheets to the larger expressions and full-body acting poses.

## Palette

The production swatches and hex codes are in `color-palette.png` and `color-palette.svg`. Character shadows stay as simple hard-edged shapes; reserve soft gradients for the background and analog reproduction.
